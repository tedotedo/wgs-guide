Trio Dashboard

Manage separate Record of Discussion forms for each person in a trio test. Every form is stored independently in your browser — editing one never overwrites another.

🔒
Everything stays on this device

Each form is saved to localStorage under a unique key (rod-form-data:<id>). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser's site data removes every saved form.

🧬
Typical trio setup

For a proband (affected child) and both parents, you'll want three forms with ids like proband, mother, and father. You can also use names if you prefer — alex, sam, jamie all work.
